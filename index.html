How we choose our picks

We don’t run lab tests or set timers in our own kitchens. Instead, we cull recommendations from years of owner feedback on major retail and review platforms, plus coverage from trusted, independent product reviewers who stress-test machines over months. We look for patterns in what actually breaks: flimsy heating plates, inaccurate water cups, and poaching trays that warp after a few uses. A machine earns a spot here only if it’s praised for consistent egg doneness, easy cleanup, and long-term durability across hundreds of user reviews. We also read between the lines of negative reviews to understand if failures are rare defects or widespread design flaws. Price alone never earns a pick, value does. That means a budget model has to perform well enough to justify its low cost, and a premium model has to offer meaningful improvements (like larger capacity or more precise doneness control) rather than just a prettier shell. We update our picks every year to reflect new models and evolving owner experiences.
