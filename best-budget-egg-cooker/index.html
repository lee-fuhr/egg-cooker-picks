Egg cookers are one of the few countertop appliances that actually save you time and mess, they handle boiling, poaching, and often scrambling with the push of a button. For most households, a budget-friendly model does everything you need without wasted counter space or complicated menus. When choosing, focus on capacity (how many eggs it cooks at once), the included cooking trays (poached vs. hard-boiled), and the ease of cleaning. Noise level and auto-shutoff matter too, but for a budget pick, reliable, consistent results are the top priority.
