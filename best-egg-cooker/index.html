Quick answer

The Dash Rapid Egg Cooker is our top pick for its unbeatable combination of speed, consistency, and value.

If you want a larger capacity and a more polished design, the Cupertino Pure Indulgence Egg Cooker is an excellent runner-up.

Egg cookers take the guesswork out of boiling, poaching, and even scrambling eggs, delivering consistent results in minutes without hovering over a stovetop. They are especially handy for meal-prepping a week’s worth of hard-boiled eggs or cooking a quick breakfast for the family. When choosing an egg cooker, consider capacity (how many eggs you typically cook at once), cooking options (hard, soft, poached, omelet), and ease of cleanup. Automatic shut-off and an audible alert are also convenient features that many models include. Because most egg cookers work by steaming, water measurement is critical; look for models with a clear water reservoir or measuring cup for precise results.
